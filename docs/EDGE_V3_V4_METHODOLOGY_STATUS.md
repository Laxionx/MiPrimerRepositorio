# Edge V3/V4 methodology status

Historical journals generated before the price-unit contract are contaminated by malformed simulated entries: raw MT5 spread points were applied as price displacement. Edge V3 statistical conclusions and Edge V4 causal/economic conclusions are invalid pending a complete rerun using corrected research infrastructure.

Feature provenance reviews and historical export data-quality findings are not automatically invalidated; they address different questions. The Liquidity Sweep family remains frozen, not formally rejected.

For Edge V3 strict discovery, only features available at the completed setup-bar decision timestamp may be predictors. Entry-effective risk/reward distances and their derivatives are classified `at_entry`, because they depend on the next entry bar and transaction costs; they are excluded from strict discovery. The current strict pre-decision set contains ten verified numerical features, and missing or contradictory provenance fails closed.

No feature, threshold, interaction, or strategy change may be justified from the contaminated journals. Old generated journals and reports are retained only as audit evidence and must not be used as research evidence.
